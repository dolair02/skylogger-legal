Privacy Policy

Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sky Logger mobile app for Android and iOS, a flight logbook for drone pilots. It explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

The short version

Sky Logger works fully on your device without an account. If you choose to create an account for backup and cross-device sync, we store your account details and your app data securely so it can appear on your other devices. We do not sell your data or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Information we collect

If you do not create an account, all of your logbook data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

If you choose to create an account, we collect:

  • Your account details — your email address, a username (pilot call sign), and a password. They are used to create your account and sign you in.
  • Your app data — the flights, aircraft, times, altitudes, battery levels, purposes, notes, and flight paths you log, along with your onboarding choices (pilot certification, drone type, and main goal) and which Sky Logger Pro plan you have. This is stored so it can sync to your other devices.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

How your information is used

Your account details are used to authenticate you. Your app data is used to provide cross-device sync and to unlock the features of the plan you have. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for marketing.

Subscriptions and payments

Sky Logger Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription, offered as weekly, monthly, or annual plans depending on your platform. Purchases are made through Google Play on Android and the Apple App Store on iOS. Google or Apple processes your payment and manages renewals, cancellations, and refunds; we never see or store your payment card or bank details. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Where your data is stored and who it is shared with

Account data is stored using Supabase, a cloud database and authentication provider, protected by row-level security so each user can only access their own data. Data is encrypted in transit. Supabase acts as our service provider. Subscription purchases are handled by Google or Apple as described above. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

Location

If you choose to record a flight path with GPS, the app reads your device's location only while you are actively recording and stores that path with the flight. Your location is used for no other purpose and is not shared with any third party.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can export your logbook as a CSV file from within the app, and you can edit or delete individual entries at any time. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app: open the Account tab and choose "Delete my account." This is immediate and cannot be undone. If you use the app without an account, deleting individual entries or uninstalling the app removes your data. You can also email dolair01@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of your data. We keep your account data only until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
